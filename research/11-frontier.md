### Section B: DePIN (Decentralized Physical Infrastructure Networks)

#### 5. What is DePIN?
- **Concept**: Token-incentivized networks that build real physical infrastructure
- $32B+ market by end of 2025, 48% is AI-related
- The flywheel: tokens incentivize contributors → network grows → revenue increases → token value → more contributors

#### 6. DePIN Categories
- **Nodes**:
  - Wireless & Connectivity
    - Helium (IoT + 5G — 1M+ hotspots globally)
    - WiFi Map (decentralized WiFi sharing)
  - Compute & Storage
    - Filecoin (incentivized storage, 22+ EiB capacity)
    - Arweave (permanent storage)
    - Render (GPU rendering)
    - Akash (cloud compute)
  - Mapping & Geospatial
    - Hivemapper (decentralized Google Maps — dashcam mining)
    - DIMO (vehicle data network)
  - Energy
    - PowerLedger (peer-to-peer energy trading)
    - Glow (solar energy certificates)
  - Sensors & IoT
    - WeatherXM (decentralized weather stations)
    - Geodnet (RTK GNSS network)
  - CDN & Bandwidth
    - Theta (decentralized video delivery)
    - Fleek (decentralized edge compute)

### Section C: Identity & Reputation

#### 7. Decentralized Identity (DID)
- **Nodes**:
  - What is self-sovereign identity? (you own your data, not platforms)
  - DID standards (W3C DID spec — did:key, did:web, did:ion, did:ethr)
  - Verifiable Credentials (VCs — provable claims without revealing all data)
  - Ethereum Name Service (ENS — human-readable addresses, .eth names)
  - Unstoppable Domains (.crypto, .wallet, .x domains)
  - Proof of Personhood
    - Worldcoin (iris scan, World ID — controversial but largest)
    - Gitcoin Passport (multi-credential scoring)
    - Anima (face liveness)

#### 8. On-Chain Reputation
- **Nodes**:
  - Soulbound Tokens (SBTs — non-transferable identity tokens)
  - On-chain credentials (POAPs, Galxe campaigns, talent badges)
  - Credit scoring on-chain (Spectral, Cred Protocol)
  - Attestations (EAS — Ethereum Attestation Service)

#### 9. Account Abstraction & Smart Accounts
- **Nodes**:
  - ERC-4337 (account abstraction standard)
  - Smart contract wallets (programmable, recoverable, gasless)
  - Passkey wallets (login with fingerprint/face)
  - Social recovery (friends help you recover your wallet)
  - Session keys (pre-approve actions without signing each one)
