# 10 — Applications: NFTs, Gaming, Social, Payments

> "Beyond finance — the consumer-facing use cases that bring the next billion users."

## Why This Canvas Matters

DeFi is for finance people. Applications are for *everyone*. NFTs, gaming, social media, and payments are where crypto actually touches normal people's lives. This pillar covers the demand side — what people actually *do* with crypto beyond trading.

---

## Canvas Structure

### Main Node: "Applications"
*"Consumer-facing crypto: digital ownership, gaming, social networks, and everyday payments"*

### Sub-Nodes (5 categories):

#### 1. NFTs & Digital Ownership
- **Nodes**:
  - What is an NFT? (unique on-chain tokens, ERC-721, ERC-1155)
  - PFP Collections (Bored Apes, CryptoPunks, Pudgy Penguins → IP brand play)
  - Art & Generative Art (Art Blocks, fxhash, Bright Moments)
  - Music NFTs (Sound.xyz, Catalog, Royal — artist revenue)
  - NFT Marketplaces (OpenSea, Blur, Magic Eden, Tensor)
  - NFTFi (lending against NFTs — BendDAO, Blur Lending, NFT fractionalization)
  - Ordinals & BRC-20 (Bitcoin NFTs — inscriptions on satoshis)
  - Dynamic NFTs (change based on conditions — Chainlink VRF)
  - Phygital NFTs (physical item + digital twin)
  - Where are we now? (2021 hype → 2023 crash → 2024-25 utility focus)

#### 2. Gaming & Metaverse
- **Nodes**:
  - Play-to-Earn origins (Axie Infinity — $9.5B in 2021, then collapse)
  - Play-and-Earn evolution (sustainable gaming economics)
  - Fully On-Chain Games (Dark Forest, Loot, Autonomous Worlds)
  - Blockchain gaming infrastructure (Immutable X, Ronin, Beam)
  - In-game economies and asset ownership
  - AAA web3 games in development (Illuvium, Star Atlas, Shrapnel)
  - Game SDKs (Unity + web3, Unreal Engine + web3)
  - Virtual worlds (Decentraland, The Sandbox, Otherside)
  - Gaming chains (Ronin, Immutable X, Beam/Merit Circle, Xai)

#### 3. Social & Creator Economy (SocialFi)
- **Nodes**:
  - Decentralized Social Protocols
    - Farcaster (Ethereum-native social, frames, channels — $5B sector)
    - Lens Protocol (social graph, on-chain — Polygon/cross-chain)
    - Bluesky (AT Protocol, decentralized Twitter alternative)
    - Nostr (minimalist protocol, Bitcoin-aligned)
  - Social tokens and creator tokens
    - Friend.tech (Base, social tokens for creators — rise and fall)
    - Rally, Bonfire
  - Content platforms
    - Mirror (decentralized blogging/publishing)
    - Paragraph (newsletter platform)
  - Social graphs (portable, user-owned data)
  - Why SocialFi matters (censorship resistance, data ownership, creator monetization)

#### 4. Payments (Enhanced)
- **Existing canvas** — link and enrich
- **Additional context**:
  - $46T annual stablecoin volume (larger than Visa)
  - Merchant integration (Stripe crypto payouts, PayPal PYUSD)
  - Remittances (lower fees than Western Union, instant settlement)
  - x402 standard (HTTP payment protocol)
  - Lightning Network for Bitcoin payments
  - Tron as stablecoin payment rail (50%+ of USDT)
  - On/off ramps (MoonPay, Transak, Ramp)

#### 5. Memecoins & Culture
- **Nodes**:
  - What are memecoins? (community-driven, no utility, pure speculation + culture)
  - Major memecoins (DOGE, SHIB, PEPE, WIF, BONK)
  - Pump.fun (Solana memecoin launcher — millions of tokens created)
  - Memecoin culture (CT, communities, virality)
  - Token launchpad mechanics (bonding curves, liquidity bootstrapping)
  - The debate: are memecoins good or bad for crypto?
  - Celebrity tokens (Trump, Milady, etc.)
