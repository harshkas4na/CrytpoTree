# 12 — Regulation: The Global Legal Landscape

> "The rules being written today will shape crypto for the next decade."

## Why This Canvas Matters

Regulation is the single biggest external force acting on crypto. MiCA in Europe, the GENIUS Act in the US, and licensing frameworks in Dubai, Singapore, and Hong Kong are creating a new reality. Every user — from beginner to institution — needs to understand the regulatory landscape.

---

## Canvas Structure

### Main Node: "Regulation"
*"How governments worldwide are regulating crypto: stablecoin frameworks, securities law, AML, and the path to clarity"*

### Sub-Nodes (5 categories):

#### 1. US Regulatory Landscape
- **Nodes**:
  - SEC (Securities and Exchange Commission)
    - Shift from enforcement-first to rules-first (2025+)
    - The Howey Test (is this token a security?)
    - ETF approvals (Bitcoin ETFs Jan 2024, Ethereum ETFs 2024)
    - SAB 121 rescission (banks can custody crypto)
  - CFTC (Commodity Futures Trading Commission)
    - Bitcoin and ETH as commodities
    - Derivatives regulation
  - GENIUS Act (first US stablecoin framework, signed July 2025)
    - Reserve requirements, audit standards
    - Stablecoin issuer licensing
  - FIT21 (Financial Innovation and Technology Act)
  - IRS crypto tax rules (cost basis reporting, broker requirements)

#### 2. European Regulation (MiCA)
- **Nodes**:
  - MiCA (Markets in Crypto-Assets Regulation — full effect Jan 2025)
  - Licensing framework for exchanges, custodians, issuers
  - Stablecoin rules (reserve requirements, volume caps)
  - E-money token (EMT) vs Asset-Referenced Token (ART) classification
  - DORA (Digital Operational Resilience Act)
  - Enforcement: €540M+ in fines by Nov 2025
  - Impact on industry (compliance costs, market structure)

#### 3. Asia & Middle East
- **Nodes**:
  - Singapore (MAS licensing — payment token services)
  - Hong Kong (dual licensing — retail + institutional)
  - Japan (JFSA — progressive crypto regulation)
  - South Korea (Virtual Asset User Protection Act)
  - Dubai / UAE (VARA — Virtual Asset Regulatory Authority)
  - India (30% crypto tax, uncertain regulatory status)
  - China (complete ban on crypto trading, but blockchain R&D encouraged)

#### 4. Key Regulatory Themes
- **Nodes**:
  - AML/KYC (Anti-Money Laundering, Know Your Customer)
  - Travel Rule (sharing sender/receiver info for transfers >$1K)
  - Securities classification (when is a token a security?)
  - DeFi regulation challenges (how do you regulate smart contracts?)
  - DAO legal status (entity classification, liability)
  - Privacy coin regulation (delisting from exchanges in some jurisdictions)
  - Stablecoin regulation (the first area of global consensus)
  - NFT regulation (when are they securities? when are they commodities?)

#### 5. Compliance Infrastructure
- **Nodes**:
  - Chainalysis (blockchain analytics, govt contracts)
  - Elliptic (transaction monitoring)
  - TRM Labs (compliance platform)
  - Fireblocks (institutional custody with compliance)
  - Circle (USDC — compliance-first stablecoin)
  - KYC/AML providers (Jumio, Onfido, Synaps)
